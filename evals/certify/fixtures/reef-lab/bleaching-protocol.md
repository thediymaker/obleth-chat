# Reef Lab: thermal bleaching assay (protocol RL-P-07, revision 3)

Fragments rest for 7 days at 27.0 °C before the assay.

Heat stress: ramp 0.5 °C per hour to 32.5 °C, then hold for 72 hours.

Photosynthetic efficiency is read with the PAM fluorometer after 30 minutes of dark
adaptation. A fragment with Fv/Fm below 0.45 at the end of the hold is scored as
bleached. Between 0.45 and 0.55 it is scored as stressed.

Each run needs three fragments per colony and one unheated control tank.
